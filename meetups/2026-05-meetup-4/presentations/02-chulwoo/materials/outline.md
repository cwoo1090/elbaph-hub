# Chulwoo May 2026 Talk Outline

Audience: Elbaph members and guests at the 4th meetup on 2026-05-31.

Title: Building in public, Maple

Thesis: Maple started as a personal LLM Wiki tool for studying robot hardware, but building in public surfaced a real B2B use case faster than planned.

Deck: `index.html`

## Flow

1. Maple capture first
   - Show the real app with the robot hardware wiki.
   - Make the product concrete before explaining the story.

2. Starting point
   - I was using LLM Wiki with Obsidian + Codex to study robot hardware.
   - It worked, but maintaining structure was hard.

3. Friction
   - Codex had too much freedom.
   - Wiki structure, source policy, naming, and maintenance drifted too easily.

4. Why app
   - I made Maple to put rails around the workflow.
   - Import, build wiki, ask wiki, maintain.

5. Building in public
   - I posted the app-building process in threads.
   - The thread became a discovery loop, not just marketing.

6. Unexpected customer
   - An academy teaching International Baccalaureate (IB) contacted me.
   - They wanted to use Maple to build lecture/problem-set wikis for students.

7. Contract
   - We made a contract with initial setup fee + monthly subscription.
   - They valued the result more than the implementation difficulty from my side.

8. Public wiki
   - Builders use Maple app.
   - Students use the published website.
   - I am testing this with my robot hardware wiki.

9. Lesson 1
   - I made it for personal study, but customers found new use cases.
   - VC reviewers and people wanting team/research wikis also reached out.
   - Building in public means customer acquisition + direct needs discovery.

10. Lesson 2
    - There is a large gap in practical AI usage.
    - What takes me a few days can be very valuable to others.
    - This arbitrage may last for a while.

11. Next
    - Ship the IB wiki workflow.
    - Continue testing public wiki with robot hardware.
    - Keep posting the build process.
