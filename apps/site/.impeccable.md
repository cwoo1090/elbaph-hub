# Impeccable Design Context — Elbaph Site

## Design Context

### Users
Prospective members and guest speakers who are ambitious, technically sophisticated builders — mostly Korean, bilingual. They arrive via word of mouth or a direct invite. They're evaluating: "Is this crew worth my time?" The site needs to answer that in seconds with credibility, not persuasion.

### Brand Personality
**Ambitious · Intellectual · Bold**

Voice: direct, no-filler, high-standards. Like a small group of people who don't need to prove themselves — but what they've built speaks clearly. Korean cultural context matters: warmth and camaraderie coexist with intense drive.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light mode — warm off-white `#faf9f6`, not pure white. Clean, editorial, easy on Korean text.
- **References**: YC/Pioneer.app energy (selectivity, credibility, "we pick the best people") executed with Linear/Vercel's typographic precision and restraint.
- **Anti-references**: No startup landing page clichés — no gradient hero sections, no floating cards, no testimonial carousels, no stock photography, no purple/blue brand colors.
- **Pattern**: Editorial grid — `gap-px border` cells, high contrast text, generous whitespace. Sections feel like pages in a well-designed annual report, not a SaaS homepage.

### Design Principles
1. **Credibility over persuasion** — Show the people, the work, the ideas. Trust comes from substance, not sales copy.
2. **Every pixel earns its place** — No decorative elements that don't carry meaning. Restraint is a signal of confidence.
3. **Typographic hierarchy does the work** — Bold display for headlines, muted labels, clear body text. No icons, no illustrations.
4. **Korean-first, English-equal** — Both locales must feel native, not translated. Spacing and sizing must accommodate Korean character density.
5. **Bold but not loud** — The ambition is in the ideas and the people, not the visual effects. Motion and color should be purposeful and rare.
